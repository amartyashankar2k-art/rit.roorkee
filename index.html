Instant Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick resolutions to common queries regarding fee deposits, hostel check-ins, library borrowing limits, and placement drive eligibility.

Students can deposit tuition and hostel fees securely through the RIT ERP Student Portal via Net Banking, UPI, or Credit/Debit Card. Alternatively, demand drafts in favor of "Roorkee Institute of Technology" payable at Roorkee can be submitted at the accounts branch (Admin Block - Ground Floor).
For security, students must report back to campus by 7:30 PM (Winter) and 8:00 PM (Summer). Hostel biometric attendance is recorded daily at 8:30 PM. Any outstation leave requires a digital or written gate-pass approved by the respective hostel warden and parent confirmation.
Eligible students (minimum 60% aggregate without active backlogs for tier-1 companies) are enrolled via the Training & Placement Cell portal. Drive announcements, syllabus, and test links are dispatched to student registered emails and published on the T&P Notice Board.
Undergraduate students can issue up to 4 books for 14 days, and Postgraduate/M.Tech scholars can issue up to 6 books. Renewals can be completed twice online via OPAC if the title is not on hold for another member.
You can submit a ticket above under the "Campus Wi-Fi & ERP Login" category or visit the IT Infrastructure Helpdesk (Computer Center, 2nd Floor, Academic Block B) along with your college identity card.
